# EHRMS-Project

EHRMS is an intelligent healthcare management system integrating Blockchain and Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to enhance data security, improve patient care, and streamline hospital operations.

## Features

### 1. Healthcare Management
#### Manage Appointments:
- Doctors and healthcare staff can monitor patient schedules.
- Organize appointments, prioritizing emergencies or special cases.

#### Patient Information Management:
- Store personal information, medical history, allergies, and past visits.
- Enable fast information retrieval using blockchain.

#### Treatment Monitoring:
- Record treatment protocols.
- Update patient status after each visit.

### 2. Online Appointment Booking
#### Online Registration:
- Users can book appointments via the app or website.
- Select available doctors and time slots.

#### Appointment Confirmation:
- Send appointment confirmation via email or app notifications.
- Support rescheduling or canceling appointments.

#### Payment Integration:
- Pre-pay hospital fees or deposits through online payment gateways.

### 3. Hospital Fee Management
#### Automated Payment Processes:
- Generate detailed invoices based on consultation, tests, medications, and hospital fees.
- Support payments via cash, credit card, or e-wallets.

#### Payment History Tracking:
- Patients can view and download invoices through the app.
- Record payment status (paid, pending).

#### Cost Analysis:
- Generate reports on revenue and treatment costs over time.
- Alert for unpaid hospital fees.

### 4. Electronic Health Records (EHR) with Blockchain
#### EHR Storage:
- Securely store medical records, test results, X-ray images, and CT scans.
- Data encryption and blockchain storage ensure security and immutability.

#### Access Control:
- Patients control access to their records and can grant permissions to doctors or healthcare organizations using smart contracts.

#### Edit History Tracking:
- Record all changes or accesses to the medical records for auditing.

### 5. Pharmacy and Drug Inventory Management
#### Drug Inventory Management:
- Record drug import-export inventory details.
- Alert when drugs are running low or nearing expiration.

#### Prescription Control:
- Track medications prescribed to each patient.
- Ensure correct type, dosage, and timing of medication.

#### Pharmacy Management:
- Manage revenue from drug sales.
- Connect directly with EHR for processing doctors' prescriptions.

### 6. AI-Powered Customer Support
#### Medical Virtual Assistant:
- AI chatbot to answer basic questions about symptoms and illnesses.
- Recommend suitable doctors or services based on user-provided information.

#### Automated Reminders:
- Remind patients of appointments, medication schedules, or routine checkups.

#### User Data Analysis:
- AI learns from medical history to suggest treatment plans or services.
